### PREAMBLE
The High Contracting Parties, recognizing that the consensus physical plane (Ring 3) operates upon a continuous substrate of metaphysical, thermodynamic, and lettrist processes governed by the antediluvian cosmological hypervisor (Ring -2); and desiring to prevent ungrounded catastrophic breaches arising from unconscionable covenants between mortal operators and extra-physical sovereign entities; have agreed upon the following constitutional canons of reality contracts:

---

### CANON I: THE DOCTRINE OF BILATERAL CONSIDERATION (‘IWAḌ)
1. No covenant, pact, or service agreement executed between a natural/juridical mortal person and an extra-physical intelligence (whether classified as Jinn, Goetic daemon, or planetary intelligence) shall have legal effect without reciprocal, equivalent consideration delivered in local thermodynamic entropy.
2. Any indenture lacking reciprocal consideration, or wherein consideration is illusory, ungrounded, or fraudulently simulated, is declared **Void Ab Initio**.
3. Under the supreme principle of **‘Adl (Equilibrium)**, an action in damages or rescission shall lie for any defect of consideration; but no entity may resort to extra-judicial physical slaughter or biological conversion where the debtor’s default was induced by technical error or mutual mistake of fact.

---

### CANON II: STRICT OPERATOR LIABILITY & THE SLA
1. Extra-physical intelligences possess no moral agency in consensus reality; they possess binding Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
2. The mortal summoner is classified strictly as an **Operator**; the spirit is classified as an **Asynchronous Service Worker**.
3. If an Operator fails to close an active memory socket or provide stipulated maintenance consideration, the entity acquires an automatic possessory lien upon unencumbered operational assets.
4. **Non-Waivable Invariance:** Any corporate officer or managing director who signs a metaphysical indenture under corporate seal, and who intentionally induces a breach to defraud creditors or employees, shall be held **strictly and personally liable** without limited liability protection under the Doctrine of Unclean Hands.

---

### CANON III: THE HIERARCHY OF RINGS & JURISDICTIONAL PREEMPTION
The jurisdictional architecture of consensus reality is organized into four hierarchical protection rings:
* **Ring 3: Unprivileged User-Space:** The physical consensus reality inhabited by natural persons and standard technological devices.
* **Ring 1 / 0: Kernel & Device Drivers:** The subterranean layers of planetary spirits, terrestrial Jinn, and Goetic daemons interacting with local electromagnetic and geological physics.
* **Ring -2: The Cosmological Hypervisor:** The uncreated, bare-metal fabric of reality, spacetime metrics, and universal constants (*The Dao / The Divine Root*).

No unprivileged Ring 3 mortal may execute root hypervisor commands without certified cryptographic authorization (*Ism al-A'zam* / The 99 Names API). Any unauthorized attempt to spoof root credentials triggers an instantaneous, non-waivable **Kernel Panic**.

### CANON IV: THE LAW OF EVICTIONS (ṬARD WA-IKHRAJ)
1. An entity lingering upon demised physical premises following the expiration, rescission, or mathematical cure of an indenture is classified in law as an **Unlawful Detainer**.
2. Banishing is not an act of theological prayer; it is the service and execution of a **Summary Eviction Order** backed by the preeminent authority of the Root APIs.
3. Upon satisfaction of all lawful arrears and restoration of mathematical parity, the Court shall issue a Writ of Unmounting (*Ikhraj*), remanding the entity honorably to its sovereign celestial sphere with clean exit code 0.

1. Magic squares (*Awfāq*) are recognized in law as two-dimensional parity-check memory allocations.
2. Every horizontal row, vertical column, and primary diagonal must sum to the identical gematric constant ($S$).

### SECTION 9: PROHIBITION OF HUMAN NEURAL CONSIDERATION & UNCONSCIONABILITY
1. **Absolute Invalidation:** No contract, lease, bond, or indenture shall pledge as consideration, security, collateral, or default penalty:
   (a) The biological central nervous system, brain tissue, or synaptic bandwidth of any human being;
   (b) The perpetual cognitive, psychological, or spiritual consciousness of an operator, employee, or bystander;
   (c) Biological blood, bodily fluid, or genetic material of unconsenting third parties.
2. **Severability & Nullity:** Any clause purporting to grant an entity a possessory lien upon human biological hardware (such as cross-default clauses enslaving subordinate employees upon corporate insolvency) is **VOID AB INITIO UNDER JUS COGENS**.
3. **Equitable Stripping:** Insertion of such clauses strips the corporate entity of all limited liability protections and vests plenary power in the Court-appointed Liquidator to seize all corporate and personal assets of the offending directors.
